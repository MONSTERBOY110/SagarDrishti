# TEAM-ROLES — SagarDrishti (6 members)

Assignment principle (from winner analysis): **role clarity over skill overlap** — hackers, designer, ideator, speaker, strategist. Every member owns something judges can ask them about.

| Role | Who | Owns (build) | Owns (competition) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead / Architect** | Team lead (user) | 3D engine (TRD M3), agent + voice (M4), offline mode (M6), final integration | Technical narrative, power-round demo driving, judge Q&A on architecture |
| **Data Engineer** | Dev @70% | Ingestion + registry (M1), API + OGC endpoints (M2), offline cube build | "Where does the data come from" answers; extensibility live-demo (add a variable on stage) |
| **Analytics Dev** | Dev @50% | Profile charts, RMSE scorecard (M5), exports/reports rendering | Metrics slide; scorecard walkthrough in evaluations |
| **Design & Story** | Beginner 1 | UI panels, colormaps/legends, visual polish, slide design (6-slide PDF + finale deck) | The "glitter lingers" factor at screening; outreach-mode demo |
| **Content & Scenarios** | Beginner 2 | Storyboards ×4 (M7), narration scripts + recordings, case-study data collection (cyclone tracks) | Opens the pitch with the story; education/outreach answers |
| **QA & Ops** | Beginner 3 | Test checklist after every merge, Playwright demo-path runs, demo laptop + spare readiness, timekeeping in finale | Failure-drill runner (kill WiFi / kill agent), backup video, logistics |

## Ground rules

1. **Ownership is real**: the owner merges their module; the lead reviews but does not silently rewrite (teaching debt is repaid before the finale, not during).
2. **Beginners pair-rotate** through the lead's modules 2h/week — by GF, each can explain the whole system in 2 minutes (judges quiz individual members).
3. **Every member has a speaking part** in the pitch and can answer: what problem, why this PS, what I built, how it helps INCOIS.
4. **Daily 15-min sync** during build phases; task board is the single source of truth; no work outside the board during the finale.
5. **≥1 female member** confirmed at roster time (SIH hard rule); 2 faculty/industry mentors registered.
6. **Mentoring rounds at GF**: QA & Ops member scribes every judge comment; lead triages into now/next/no with reasons logged (we show judges their feedback was implemented — a proven winner behavior).
